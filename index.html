1. Select a preloaded problem or start your own

Probabilities can be entered as decimals (0.25), percentages (25%), or decimal commas (0,25). They must add up to 1.

2. Payoff table

Edit any name, probability, or payoff cell. Use the remove buttons to change the number of states or alternatives. At least two states and two alternatives are required.

3. Staged filtering

An alternative is dominated if another alternative gives an equal or higher payoff in every state and a strictly higher payoff in at least one state.

How to verify the calculations

Maximax and Maximin

Maximax: choose the alternative with the largest single payoff.

Maximin: choose the alternative whose worst payoff is the highest.

Minimax regret

Subtract each alternative's payoff from the best payoff in that state. Choose the alternative with the smallest maximum regret.

Laplace and EMV

Laplace: average the payoffs assuming equal probabilities.

EMV: multiply each payoff by its state's probability and add the results.

Hurwicz

α × best payoff + (1 − α) × worst payoff.
